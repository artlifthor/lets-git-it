The Powerlifting Programming Manual is complete.

- **56 chapters** across five Parts: Foundations, Building Programs, Advanced Programming, Peaking & Competition, and Technique & Injury Management.
- **479 peer-reviewed studies**, each checked in the official DOI registry and for retractions, and listed on the [References](/lets-git-it/references/) page.
- **PAP National Records** update automatically every month on the [PAP National Records](/lets-git-it/pap-records/) page.

## What's next

From here, this page will show two kinds of updates every two months:

- **Research updates:** new meta-analyses and peer-reviewed studies on each chapter's topic, plus any retracted studies removed.
- **Coaching trends reports:** what respected programming and technique coaches are currently discussing, with an evidence check for each practice.
