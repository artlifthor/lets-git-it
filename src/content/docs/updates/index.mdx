Every two months the manual is checked against new peer-reviewed research, and what respected coaches are discussing on podcasts, YouTube and social media is reviewed against the evidence. Each update is listed here, newest first.

- **Research updates** list new studies added, retracted studies removed, and which chapters changed.
- **Coaching trends reports** summarise what well-known programming and technique coaches are currently saying, with an evidence check for each practice. They critique ideas, not people, and link every source.
