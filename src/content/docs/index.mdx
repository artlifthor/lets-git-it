## How to use this manual

The manual is organized like a textbook, in five Parts that go from basic to advanced:

1. **Foundations (Beginner):** how training works and how to coach a new lifter
2. **Building Programs (Intermediate):** periodization models and designing full training cycles
3. **Advanced Programming:** autoregulation, individual differences, and long-term planning
4. **Peaking & Competition:** tapering, making weight, attempt selection, and meet day
5. **Technique & Injury Management:** biomechanics, weak points, and training around injury

Read the Parts in order to study, or use the search bar at the top to jump straight to a topic.

## Evidence labels

Key claims in each chapter carry a label showing how well they are supported:

- **Strong evidence:** supported by meta-analyses or systematic reviews with consistent findings
- **Moderate evidence:** supported by several peer-reviewed studies
- **Emerging evidence:** limited or early peer-reviewed research; may change
- **Coaching practice:** established practice from respected coaches and authors; not yet well tested by research

Every chapter shows when it was last reviewed. The manual is checked monthly against new peer-reviewed research.
